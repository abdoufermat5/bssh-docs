bssh Teams shares connections with the people you work with. Everyone gets the same hosts in `bssh` and in the desktop app, and nobody has to pass server lists around in a chat or a wiki.

Shared connections are encrypted on your device before they leave it. The sync server stores only ciphertext and a small amount of metadata: it never sees hostnames, users or tags, and it is never in the path of your SSH connections.

## How it works

1. **Each device has its own keys.** Setting up Teams creates a signing key and an encryption key on your machine, protected by a passphrase. They never leave the device.
2. **A team has one key.** An admin gives it to each new device after checking that device's fingerprint, so the server cannot slip in a device of its own.
3. **Entries are encrypted locally.** Sharing a connection encrypts it with the team key. The server stores the result and passes it on.
4. **Teammates decrypt locally.** Each sync pulls new entries, verifies who wrote them, and adds them to your host list as `name@team`.
5. **Connections are audited.** Connecting to a team host records a signed, hash-chained event. Admins can verify the whole log on their own machine.

## What is shared

A shared connection carries its host, user, port, bastion, Kerberos setting and tags.

These are never shared:

- **Keys, key paths and passwords.** Each person connects with their own credentials or agent.
- **Your device keys and passphrase.**
- **Session recordings and terminal output.** They stay on your machine.
- **Connections you don't share.** Personal hosts stay in your local database.

## Plans

- **Free:** on the hosted service, every team has 3 seats for free, with all features.
- **Paid:** for more seats, an admin subscribes for the whole team, billed per seat, monthly or yearly.
- **Self-hosted:** run the sync server yourself; seats per team are set by the operator.

Details in [Plans and billing](/teams/plans-and-billing/). To start a team on the hosted service or ask about self-hosting, [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/abdoufermat5/bayesian-ssh/issues).
