bssh Teams is free for small teams and offers simple per-seat billing for growing organizations. Billing is managed per [organization](/teams/organizations/) by its owners.

## Seats and the free tier

Seats belong to the organization, which owns its teams. An organization's seat count is determined by:

- **Active members**: Every distinct person who has joined one of its teams. Someone in several teams takes one seat.
- **Pending invites**: Invitations issued to people who are not in the organization yet, and not yet accepted or revoked. Each one holds one seat for 7 days or until an admin revokes it with `bssh teams uninvite`. Inviting someone who is already in the organization takes no seat.

Every organization on the hosted sync server receives **3 free seats** automatically, shared by all its teams. Existing teams each became their own organization, so nothing changes for them. The free plan includes full access to end-to-end encrypted vaults, client synchronization, device verification, and signed audit logging. No credit card is required.

## Subscribing to a paid plan

When your organization exceeds 3 seats, an owner subscribes for all seats currently in use. Subscriptions are billed per seat on a monthly or yearly cycle.

<Tabs syncKey="surface">
<TabItem label="Command line">

Check the current plan, seats in use, and renewal date:

```sh
bssh org billing
```

Subscribe the organization to a paid plan:

```sh
bssh org billing --checkout --seats 8
```

To pay annually instead of monthly, add the `--yearly` flag:

```sh
bssh org billing --checkout --yearly
```

If you omit `--seats`, bssh defaults to the number of seats currently in use, rounded to at least one seat above the free plan. The command opens Stripe Checkout in your default web browser.

`<org>` is optional when you have a single organization. Otherwise name it by an id prefix or one of its teams, for example `bssh org billing platform`. `bssh teams billing <team>` still works and acts on the team's organization.

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Desktop app">

1. Open the **Teams** view from the sidebar.
2. Select your team and switch to the **Members & devices** tab.
3. Locate the **Plan and seats** section, which displays the current plan, renewal date, and a visual seat meter.
4. Click **Upgrade…** to select your seat count and billing interval (monthly or yearly).
5. Click the checkout button to open Stripe Checkout in your browser. Once payment completes, click **Refresh** on the billing card to update your plan details.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Managing your subscription

Organization owners can update payment methods, download invoices, adjust seat counts, or cancel subscriptions through the Stripe Customer Portal.

<Tabs syncKey="surface">
<TabItem label="Command line">

Open the customer billing portal in your browser:

```sh
bssh org billing --manage
```

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Desktop app">

In the team's **Members & devices** tab under **Plan and seats**, click **Manage billing**. This opens the Stripe Customer Portal in your browser.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

Checkout and the portal need an organization owner. A team admin who isn't an owner can still see the plan and seats, and gets `only an owner of the organization can manage billing` otherwise.

## Payment security

Bayesian SSH and the sync server never handle, process, or store credit card numbers. Both `--checkout` and `--manage` generate secure single-use redirect URLs to Stripe. All card transactions and billing address changes occur directly on Stripe's infrastructure.

## Read-only mode

An organization's teams enter **read-only mode** if:

- An active subscription lapses or is cancelled, and the organization has more people and pending invites than the 3 free seats.
- An administrator reduces the paid seat count in the billing portal below the organization's current member and invite count.

### What works in read-only mode

A read-only organization's teams remain functional for day-to-day operations:

- **Sync**: Teammates can run `bssh teams sync` to pull existing connections.
- **Connect**: Connecting to team hosts with `bssh connect` works without interruption.
- **Audit uploads**: Session start events continue to be signed and uploaded to the audit log.
- **Device approvals**: Admins can run `bssh teams grant` to approve keys for devices.
- **Removals**: Admins can remove members (`bssh teams remove`) or revoke pending invites (`bssh teams uninvite`).

### What is refused in read-only mode

Modifications to the shared vault are blocked:

- Adding or modifying connections (`bssh teams share`) is rejected.
- Sending new team invitations (`bssh teams invite`) is rejected.

### Restoring write access

To restore write access, an owner can either:

1. Re-subscribe the organization using `bssh org billing <org> --checkout`, or
2. Remove members or revoke pending invites until the total seat count is 3 or fewer.

## Failed payments

If a renewal payment fails, Stripe enters an automatic retry period. The subscription status changes to `past_due`.

During this retry window:

- The organization is **not** immediately placed into read-only mode. All operations, syncs, and connections continue to work.
- The desktop app displays a warning notice under **Plan and seats**: *"The last payment failed; Stripe is retrying. Update the card in Manage billing."*
- On the command line, `bssh org billing` displays `Subscription status: past_due`.
- An owner should open the billing portal (`bssh org billing --manage` or **Manage billing**) and update the payment card before the retry period expires.

## Self-hosted servers

Self-hosted sync servers run without Stripe billing integration.

- The seat limit is per organization, fixed and configured by the server operator: `DEFAULT_SEATS` when the organization is created.
- Running `bssh org billing` on a self-hosted instance shows:
  ```text
  💳 ana@example.com: 5 of 20 seats used (this server has no billing)
  ```
- The desktop application indicates that the server has no billing and that its operator sets the seat limit.
